01

The concept film

Park decided · film deferred

A 45–60 second film of the whole arc: park → expanding time → lifetime → crash back → inward time → memory corridor → park. Crude is fine — stills, Fable motion studies, generated video, rough type. This is the visual screenplay; every scene in the manifest gets its look decided here, cheaply, before any of it is engineered.

  • Decide the park — Lake Harriet, Minneapolis. Its four seasons are declared in the manifest as colour, light height, leaf density and what falls through the air.
  • Fable: type studies for the HUD, the scale labels, the two closing lines.
  • Generated plates: the park across seasons — pipeline proven in ASSETS.md; scene 04’s canopy exists in all four seasons.
  • Scene 01, the park at one second — opened 2026-09-08: the year’s world held at its opening frame through the manifest’s new hold, seen from the bench with a still camera, the thermal reading arriving on its own time as the first thing noticed. The bench itself and the small life of the second (a leaf, a blink, a bicycle) are still to make.
  • Scene 02, the ten minutes — opened 2026-09-08: the same hold with a span of the world’s time, so scroll runs ten minutes of the park’s own motion — clouds, air, water, the sun’s shadows — under a still camera, while the world starts to be read: the wind on the water, then the air through the radar. People crossing the frame are still to make.
  • Scene 03, the day — opened 2026-09-08: the hold runs a full day of the world’s clock through a lens of its own so the sun can set; the year’s world gained a night from the real sun’s altitude, a dusk that warms the horizon, and a thermal that swings with the light; the day’s arc is drawn as it happens.
  • Scene 05, the lifetime — opened 2026-09-08: the year’s world held at the August it closed on, the camera rising and drawing back as eighty years pass; the elms grow, the sun’s record stacks a figure a year into a band, and an elm counts the years as rings; the world fades for the turn. The root network, people as appearances and the changing shore are still to make.

Taken out of order on purpose: scene 04 was built first so the film has a working world to shoot against, and so the pipeline’s cost was known before a screenplay committed to it.

02

Three finished moments

Moment 1 under way

Built one at a time, each to done — beautiful, responsive, legible with almost no copy:

  • The park → one year. The pipeline proof: plates + parallax + scroll choreography + the fixed clock. Choreography built; every plate drawn in the decided style across four seasons; the instruments are what remain.
  • Ten identical minutes. The two corridors, lived vs. remembered — the centerpiece interaction. Opened 2026-09-06: the manifest declares the corridor, the two clocks’ travel, the turn and the fragments; the scene renders one procedural corridor twice, side by side, and reverses at the turn. The corridor is drawn: one bay each of wall, ceiling and floor in the boards’ style, tiled along it, and eighteen fragment cutouts on the panes (ledger session 7). The waiting clock has its one bare instrument, a seconds dial at the end of its corridor that jumps a second at a time as you scroll. The absorbed clock is read through its layers while it happens, a thermal reading with isotherms, motes in the lamplight, and a survey of measurements that thickens as the minutes pass, and looking back the densities swap. What remains for moment 2 is the look at the whole, end to end.
  • The memory corridor. Thirty days as panes of glass; alignment vs. scatter. Opened 2026-09-08: the manifest declares the days, the eye and its long lens, and two months with their windows, scatter and readings; the scene stacks a month of the same day into one sheet and scatters a month of different days across the frame, a dimension counting the days under each. The panes carry the sixty squares (ledger session 8): nine desks the same month reuses, thirty different days for the other. What remains for moment 3 is the look at the whole, end to end.

The gate: if these three moments work, the project exists. If they don’t, stop — nothing else gets built on top of them.

The rules that keep it buildable

Cheat like crazy. Choreography over polygon count. Video, plates, and depth maps wherever live 3-D isn’t earning its cost. The viewer must not be able to tell where video stops and 3-D begins.
The manifest owns time. All choreography flows from src/scenes/manifest.ts. Nothing outside it invents timing — which is also what lets an AI agent work one bounded scene without rebuilding the project.
Three moments before the universe. No connective tissue until the three hardest moments are finished and beautiful. The gate exists so three weeks aren’t spent building a universe around an idea that doesn’t land.
Tools stay in their lane. Three.js/GSAP own the experience. Fable is the motion sketchbook. Generative models manufacture ingredients. Nothing generative owns the website.
